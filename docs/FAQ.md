# Onegram FAQ

### Is this the official Telegram?

No. Onegram is an unofficial client built on Telegram's open source code and its public API. It is not affiliated with Telegram.

### Will I lose my chats?

No. Your chats live in the Telegram cloud. Onegram signs in to the same account and shows them, just like the official app does.

### Can I keep the official app?

Yes. Both can be installed at once and signed in to the same account, like Telegram on a phone and a laptop.

### Can you read my messages?

No. Messages go straight between your phone and Telegram's servers.

### Is it free?

Yes. Onegram is free. Onegram Premium is optional: it unlocks extra features for every account in the app at once and is bought in [@OnegramPremiumBot](https://t.me/OnegramPremiumBot).

### Does it work with Telegram Premium?

Yes. Everything Telegram Premium gives you keeps working.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 and newer, any processor.

### Where are the settings?

In Telegram settings there is a Onegram section. Everything Onegram adds can be turned on and off there, and there is a search.

### How does AI work?

AI features use your own API key from a provider you choose. Requests go from your phone to that provider. Nothing is used for training.

### Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Android comes first.

### How do I report a bug?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/onegramteam/onegram/issues/new/choose) or write to [@OnegramSupportBot](https://t.me/OnegramSupportBot). A screenshot and your Android version help a lot.
