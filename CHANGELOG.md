# Onegram changelog

All releases with APKs: [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/onegramteam/onegram/releases).

## 1.0.4

- Tabs of open chats
- Text recognition in photos
- Wide channel posts, long posts fold
- Hide buttons in the input field
- HD round videos and stories, voice messages up to 128 kbit/s
- Faster file uploads
- Your own name colours, menu style, transitions and icons

- Translate whole chats, free business tools
